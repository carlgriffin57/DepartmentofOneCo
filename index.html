What I build

Most businesses need the same handful of things to work online. A site people trust. A way to get in touch that actually reaches you. A few tasks handled automatically instead of by hand at 11pm.

Why one person

Hiring an agency means paying for a team. A project manager. A designer. A developer. A markup on all three. Your work passes through a lot of hands, and something gets lost each time.

With me, nothing gets handed off. I spent thirty years in software quality assurance and engineering — my whole job was catching what breaks before it ships. That habit doesn't switch off. What I hand you is tested.
